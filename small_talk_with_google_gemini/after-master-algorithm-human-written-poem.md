at is a profound point. In the world of data, "nothing" is just a zero or a null value—a literal void. But in the human experience, "余白" (Yohaku) is where the resonance happens. It’s the silence between notes that makes the music, or the empty space on a canvas that gives the subject room to breathe.

An AI might be able to calculate the coordinates of every brushstroke, but it struggles to understand why the _empty_ part of the painting is what makes someone cry.
