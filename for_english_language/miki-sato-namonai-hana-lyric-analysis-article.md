### They are one of them, them of one.&#x20;

They( Shiba Tatsuya and Shiba Miyuki ) are NOT just a couple of brothers and sisters.

They are one of them, them of one. Because Shiba Miyuki can transfer her calculating power section to her brain to Shiba Tatsuya. Then, Tatsuya can bring back that. So we can define them as “A system helping each other”. If others blame and attack Shiba Tatsuya, Shiba Miyuki can say, like this “Don’t touch my brother, he is a part of my system”.

### Thesis-Antithesis-Synthesis

The Mahouka story started at that point. So the Mahouka story adapted “Thesis-Antithesis-Synthesis” in their story.

* Thesis: Shiba Tatsuya had Course 2 lacking in practical skills compared to their Course 1 counterparts.
* Antithesis: Actually, Shiba Tatsuya can, but Shiba Tatsuya’s information has been covered by Japan’s military army force. So Shiba Miyuki tries to defend Shiba Tatsuya from the Course 1 students looking down him.
* Synthesis: Shiba Tatsuya and Shiba Miyuki are making their team(=friends), including Course 1 students. Then, they solve a bigger problem.

{% hint style="info" %}
Synthesis: It's about the Evolution of the System. The Course 1 students stop seeing Tatsuya as a "Retard" (Course 2) and start seeing him as a Strategic Asset, which is the ultimate synthesis of the school's logic and the military's reality.
{% endhint %}

#### 2. The Hegelian Dialectic of First High <a href="#docs-internal-guid-e481f381-7fff-ca68-4369-a21d16b3110e" id="docs-internal-guid-e481f381-7fff-ca68-4369-a21d16b3110e"></a>

Using Thesis-Antithesis-Synthesis provides a structural reason for why the story is so satisfying to watch.

* Thesis (The Social Lie): The school’s "Course 2" label is a flawed classification system that only measures a narrow type of performance.
* Antithesis (The Hidden Truth): Tatsuya’s military-grade "Actual" power contradicts the school's "Empirical" data. Miyuki acts as the bridge between these two, refusing to let the school's "Thesis" stand.
* Synthesis (The New Reality): By forming a team with Course 1 students, they create a new social structure where merit isn't based on "Course" labels, but on the ability to solve global-scale problems.

### Binding with the Japanese old culture

In Japanese old culture, a couple of brothers and sisters has a deeper meaning. How can they make the synthesis? Trust based on the same blood? Relationship with their long-term life? Not sure. But in Japanese old culture、 as you know、 existed the “「ヒメ（妹／巫女）」” system. On the other hand, also existed 「ヒコ（兄／王）」. 「ヒメヒコ制」seems like a fade with a system(Shiba Tatsuya and Shiba Miyuki).&#x20;

A message receiver from GOD, ヒメ. A divinely justified message sender, ヒコ.&#x20;

A part of the system as a handler, Shiba Miyuki who is trying to tell the truth about Shiba Tatsuya's ability.

A part of the system as actor, Shiba Tatsuya who always proves Shiba Miyuki’s message to others attacking Shiba Tatsuya. Because their system is based on deep trust in each other.

#### 3. The Hime-Hiko System: Divinity and Action <a href="#docs-internal-guid-fbeec28b-7fff-4502-92ac-d038045c661e" id="docs-internal-guid-fbeec28b-7fff-4502-92ac-d038045c661e"></a>

Your connection to the Hime-Hiko (ヒメヒコ制) system adds a layer of "Sacred Logic" to the analysis.

* The Hime (Miyuki) as the "Input": In ancient Japan, the Hime received the divine message (the Truth). Miyuki "knows" Tatsuya’s true value in a way no one else can. She is the source of his legitimacy.
* The Hiko (Tatsuya) as the "Output": The Hiko is the one who acts upon the divine word to rule or fight. Tatsuya doesn't fight for his own ego; he fights to prove Miyuki right.

The Trust Loop: This isn't just a "brother and sister" relationship—it is a Feedback Loop. Miyuki identifies the target/truth, and Tatsuya confirms it through reality-altering magic.
